Flood Warning & River Level Systems

Scalable rainfall, drainage, river-level, and flood warning networks built specifically for high-intensity tropical monsoon zones, coastal plains, and dense urban catchments.

Early Detection for Operational Resilience

AUREX flood warning stations combine high-accuracy non-contact radar level sensors with telemetry logic to detect rising water lines instantly. Operating on dual cellular and satellite networks, they ensure data transmission remains active even during extreme storm events when local power or telecom infrastructure fails.

These systems integrate with civic databases, sirens, and cloud alerting systems to give municipal authorities and industrial plant managers vital hours to deploy barriers, clear drains, and evacuate fields.

Key Technical Capabilities

Designed for extreme tropical weather and high-load communication requirements.

Non-Contact Radar Sensing

Radar level sensors measure water lines continuously from bridges or gantries, avoiding physical contact with debris, mud, or rapid river currents.

Redundant Telemetry

Includes multi-band 4G/LTE connectivity with fallback to satellite or LPWAN (LoRaWAN) to guarantee transmission under all weather scenarios.

Automated Local Sirens

Stations can trigger nearby strobe lights and warning horns locally when critical water levels are breached, bypassing software latencies.
