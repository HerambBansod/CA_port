Environmental Intelligence Solutions

AUREX designs, integrates, and deploys high-availability monitoring systems built to withstand the rigours of tropical, coastal, and remote operating environments across the Asia-Pacific region.

Automatic Weather Stations

Our Automatic Weather Stations (AWS) are engineered for reliable, continuous measurement of core meteorological parameters in high-humidity, coastal, and remote tropical conditions. Equipped with low-power dataloggers, secure telemetry, and industrial-grade sensors.

  • Temperature & Humidity
  • Barometric Pressure
  • Wind Speed & Direction
  • Solar Radiation
  • Telemetry (4G, LoRa, Satellite)
  • Solar Power Systems

Hydrometeorological Systems

We provide integrated surface water monitoring, river gauge stations, and coastal tide systems designed for catchments, municipalities, and infrastructure managers. These systems support localized flood risk assessment, dam safety operations, and drainage intelligence.

  • Radar & Ultrasonic Level Sensors
  • Tipping Bucket Rain Gauges
  • Water Quality Multiprobe Integration
  • Alert & Siren Controllers
View Flood Warning Solutions

Lightning Detection & Warning

AUREX deploys precision lightning detection sensors and regional alert platforms that track cloud-to-ground and cloud-to-cloud electromagnetic activity. Perfect for petrochemical sites, open-cast mines, golf courses, and outdoor transit operators requiring early safety warnings.

  • Severe Storm Warnings
  • Electromagnetic Field Mills
  • Audio-Visual Siren Relays
  • Precision Local Coordinates

Aviation Weather Systems

Providing airports and helipads with compliant meteorological observing systems (AWOS). Our platforms display real-time wind shear alerts, visibility readings, cloud ceiling heights, and pressure settings crucial for pilot safety and air traffic operations.

  • Runway Visual Range (RVR)
  • Ceilometer Cloud Height
  • Barometric Pressure Systems
  • ICAO Compliant Reporting

Renewable Energy Intelligence

Optimising operations for wind and solar power generation across Asia. Our monitoring systems track global horizontal irradiance (GHI), plane of array (POA) solar values, wind velocity, and ambient parameters to forecast output, plan grid compliance, and verify performance indices.

  • ISO Class A Pyranometers
  • Soiling Monitoring Systems
  • Performance Ratio (PR) Metrics
  • SCADA Integration
