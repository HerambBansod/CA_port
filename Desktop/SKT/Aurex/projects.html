Project Experience

AUREX has supported and participated in a wide range of meteorological, environmental, and infrastructure monitoring initiatives across Asia and the Asia-Pacific region.

Our project experience spans government agencies, transportation networks, utilities, renewable energy operators, industrial facilities, and research organizations requiring reliable environmental intelligence and operational resilience.

Our work includes the deployment and integration of automatic weather station networks, hydrometeorological monitoring systems, lightning detection technologies, flood and rainfall monitoring solutions, and aviation weather platforms designed for both urban and remote environments. These projects are often tailored to meet the operational requirements of tropical climates, coastal regions, mountainous terrain, and rapidly developing infrastructure corridors throughout Asia.

Through strategic collaborations and regional partnerships, AUREX continues to contribute to projects that improve public safety, strengthen infrastructure resilience, enhance operational efficiency, and support sustainable development initiatives across Asia.

Featured Deployments

Highlighting our recent engineering achievements across key environmental sectors in Asia.

Hydromet / Early Warning

Flood Monitoring Network

Real-time rainfall and river-level monitoring for flood preparedness and public safety. We designed, installed, and integrated radar sensors along vulnerable river corridors, transmitting real-time alerts to municipal control centers during monsoon phases.

Coastal / Environmental

Coastal Observation System

Monitoring solutions for coastal, tropical, and high-humidity operating environments. Developed to withstand marine atmospheric corrosion, this system aggregates temperature, humidity, salinity, and wind shear metrics for coastal infrastructure.

Aviation Weather

Airport Weather Platform

Integrated weather intelligence to support safer and more informed aviation operations. Complete AWOS platform delivery featuring runway sensor grids, barometric pressure calibration, and secure cloud interfaces for real-time traffic coordination.
