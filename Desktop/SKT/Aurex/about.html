About AUREX

AUREX is a Singapore-based weather technology company delivering advanced environmental monitoring hardware, meteorological intelligence, and data-driven solutions across Asia.

Weather Intelligence for Operational Resilience

We specialize in the design, integration, deployment, and support of weather monitoring systems that help governments, infrastructure operators, utilities, aviation stakeholders, renewable energy developers, and enterprise customers make smarter operational decisions in rapidly changing environments.

With operations and partnerships throughout Southeast Asia, South Asia, and the broader Asia-Pacific region, AUREX combines modern sensing technologies, real-time data acquisition, and intelligent analytics to provide reliable weather and environmental insights where they matter most.

Inspired by the evolving capabilities of modern meteorological and environmental technology providers in Singapore and internationally, AUREX focuses on delivering scalable, high-reliability systems backed by responsive regional support and long-term customer partnerships.
